# Changelog

## v1.1.0 — 2026-11-17

- Fixed overlapping text in the act prologues ("A thousand years ago…"),
  the boss lines in battle and the save/load screen title: these used a fixed
  letter spacing that ignored the new font.
- Translated the opening staff cards.
- Shop gear stats show the right number after At/Df/Sp (was "/Df ;0").
- Scrolls renamed: Young Grass → Leaf, Lightning Flash → Flash. The scroll
  grid in menus and battle shows short names with a gap between them.
- Zipangu is now Jipang.
- Save slots read "Lv3" instead of "3Lv/".
- "Warp" for the teleport menu entry.

## v1.0.2 — 2026-11-11

- Translated the remaining menu and message text: item, field and battle
  messages (including Takako's letters and Kinu's fortunes), shop and
  facility names, the travel menu, equipment labels, battle status labels
  and target prompts.
- Fixed overlapping text in item/gear info lines and the shop header.
- Shop Yes/No prompts no longer push the choice out of the box.
- Battle menus use "Manji"/"Gokur." where the full names overflowed.

## v1.0.1 — 2026-11-10

- Fixed the "DBZ bug": after long conversations the game's memory was
  overwritten (sudden poison, deaths, revivals, level jumps, broken event
  flags). A save that was already damaged can't be repaired.
- Translated the staff roll, including the clear time at the end.
- Translated artwork: the Manjimaru and Kabuki Danjuro name plaques, the
  "Manto 2" TV advert, the Kasa-iwa Hermit's drawing and Duke Pepe's wanted
  poster.

## v0.9 beta — 2026-11-10

First public release.

- All story and NPC dialogue (11,661 lines) translated and context-reviewed.
- All 189 cutscene subtitles translated and refitted: lines that share a row
  in the Japanese layout are repositioned so the English never overlaps.
- New variable-width font for the dialogue and menus.
- Battles: commands, messages, enemy/item/spell/art/equipment names, the
  status panel (Ex/Lv/HP/TP/At/Df/Sp), status effects and AI tactics.
- Field menus: command menu, status/list screens, items, gear, scrolls,
  techniques, shops, inns and storage.
- Place names in the menu header, the province-select map and the world-map
  labels.
- Title screen, New/Load menu, and the save/load screens with all of their
  messages and buttons.

Known issues: the whole game has not yet been played start to finish on the
patched ROM; some stylized artwork and the staff roll remain in Japanese; a
few small menu boxes use short forms (Gear/Item/Scrl, Manji, Gokur., Nxt).
