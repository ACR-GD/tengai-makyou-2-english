# Changelog

## v0.9 beta — 2026-11-10

First public release.

- All story and NPC dialogue (11,661 lines) translated and context-reviewed.
- All 189 cutscene subtitles translated and refitted: lines that share a row
  in the Japanese layout are repositioned so the English never overlaps.
- New variable-width font for the dialogue and menus.
- Battles: commands, messages, enemy/item/spell/art/equipment names, the
  status panel (Ex/Lv/HP/TP/At/Df/Sp), status effects and AI tactics.
- Field menus: command menu, status/list screens, items, gear, scrolls,
  techniques, shops, inns and storage.
- Place names in the menu header, the province-select map and the world-map
  labels.
- Title screen, New/Load menu, and the save/load screens with all of their
  messages and buttons.

Known issues: the whole game has not yet been played start to finish on the
patched ROM; some stylized artwork and the staff roll remain in Japanese; a
few small menu boxes use short forms (Gear/Item/Scrl, Manji, Gokur., Nxt).
